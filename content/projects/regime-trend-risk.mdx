# Market Regime Modeling for Systematic Trading

Built walk forward regime detection models using HMM, GARCH, GMM and Markov Switching on 20 years of US equity market data. Developed regime driven strategies with costs, achieving Sharpe 0.70 and reducing max drawdown from 55% to 27% vs SPY.
